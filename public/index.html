01 About the Founder

Counsel who explains the law — and fights for your rights.

Sabah Cheema founded SA Chambers on a simple conviction: every client deserves to understand their case, their options and their costs — without jargon and without delay. Her practice combines careful preparation of pleadings with steady advocacy in court.

An Advocate of the High Court based in Lahore, she advises and represents clients in tax, family and divorce, property, corporate and succession matters — with particular attention to women and families navigating sensitive disputes, businesses dealing with the FBR, and overseas Pakistanis who need a trusted representative at home.

          03 How We Work

          A clear path from first call to final order

          Court matters in Pakistan can feel slow and uncertain. We keep you informed at each stage, so you always know what happens next.

          1. 01

            Consultation

            Tell us what happened — in person, by phone, or on a video call. We identify the legal issue and the right forum.

          2. 02

            Documents & Strategy

            We review your papers — nikahnama, fard, agreements, tax notices, company records — and set out options, timelines and fees.

          3. 03

            Notice / Filing

            Legal notice, suit, petition, tax appeal or reply — drafted carefully and filed before the competent forum.

          4. 04

            Hearings & Updates

            We appear on every date and update you after each hearing, including the next date and what is required.

          5. 05

            Resolution

            Settlement, decree, order or appeal — and execution of the result, so the outcome actually reaches you.

          04 Overseas Pakistanis

          Abroad? Your legal matters in Pakistan are in safe hands.

          Whether you live in the Gulf, the UK, Europe or North America, you should not have to fly home for every hearing. We act for overseas clients remotely, with video consultations and regular written updates.

          • Property disputes, illegal possession and recovery of family land

          • Drafting and guidance on Special / General Power of Attorney attested abroad

          • Tax status and filing for non-resident Pakistanis
